Industry Insights

Why Modular

Off-site building methods engineered for speed, cost predictability, quality, and sustainability.

Modular construction is an off-site building method where the structure is built in separate sections or modules, typically in a manufacturing plant, using a tightly controlled assembly line process. The buildings are designed to the same model building codes and standards and incorporate identical construction materials to those that are used in facilities which are primarily site-built.

After manufacturing, the modules are transported to the construction site where they are assembled together on an appropriate foundation system yielding a building that is comparable to a site-built structure but is completed in a significantly reduced time frame.

Speed of Construction

The modular building process provides for a simultaneous overlap of the activities that take place off-site in the manufacturing plant and on-site while preparing the site for the building installation. Since the structure is being built in the factory and doesn't wait for the site preparation to take place or is subjected to weather delays, a permanent or relocatable modular building can be completed 25–40% faster than the time required for typical site-built methods.

Lower Building Costs

The modular building process can yield 10–25% lower design and construction costs, and the compressed schedule helps reduce the overall project general conditions and financing required. The approach provides for a more predictable and streamlined process with better coordination to reduce costly mistakes and material waste. The vertical integration of many modular companies also provides for a greater degree of value engineering to meet a client's budget on a project.

Superior Construction Quality

Modular buildings utilize the same materials and are built to the same codes, standards, and architectural specifications as site construction methods. Quality improvements are recognized through the factory assembly process which leads to greater efficiencies, consistency, and safety, while established protocols for receiving, rejecting, and storing materials also eliminate issues with poor or damaged components. Permanent modular buildings can be designed to match existing structures, and after installation and finish-out are often indistinguishable from site-built structures.

Sustainability

The use of factory-controlled manufacturing is also a greener process that generates less waste, reduces on-site disturbances, and allows for tighter control of the entire process. There is less material waste and greater use of recycling of building materials, and with relocatable buildings they can be dismantled and relocated or refurbished for a new or secondary use.

Modular Solutions

Two Types of Modular Construction

Whether you need flexible, relocatable swing space or a lasting, code-compliant commercial facility, Q Modular delivers both temporary and permanent construction methods tailored to your timeline and budget.

Temporary Modular Construction

Designed for speed, relocatability, and operational flexibility without long-term capital commitment.

Temporary modular buildings—commonly referred to as relocatable buildings—are engineered to be deployed, occupied, and subsequently moved or reconfigured as your organizational needs change. This approach is ideal for businesses and institutions requiring immediate space with flexible leasing or purchase options.

  • Rapid Deployment: Fabricated off-site and installed up to 40% faster than site-built structures.
  • Flexible Financing: Accessible through customized short- or long-term lease, rental, or purchase agreements.
  • Relocatable & Reusable: Can be dismantled, relocated, or refurbished for secondary applications with minimal site impact.
  • Common Uses: Jobsite field offices, modular classrooms, swing spaces, emergency clinics, and temporary sales centers.

Permanent Modular Construction

Engineered to identical commercial building codes, delivering permanent asset value on an accelerated schedule.

Permanent Modular Construction (PMC) is an innovative building method where factory-assembled volumetric modules are set onto a permanent foundation. Once completed, permanent modular buildings are indistinguishable from site-built real estate, delivering decades of durability, architectural beauty, and energy efficiency.

  • Full Code Compliance: Built to the International Building Code (IBC) and all applicable state and local commercial codes.
  • True Capital Asset: Designed for a 50+ year service life, offering identical appraisal and depreciation benefits.
  • Architectural Freedom: Multi-story capabilities with custom brick, glass, stucco, and architectural panel facades.
  • Common Uses: Multi-story commercial offices, healthcare facilities, schools, government complexes, and banks.
